## What

An ad-research workflow splits Meta video ads into shots and rates them.

## How Jev fits

Maxfusion handles video understanding; Jev scores derived shot information and contributes to an overall rating.

## Why and impact

Author reports 450+ ads processed in under three minutes and about 1.5 seconds for shot scoring.
