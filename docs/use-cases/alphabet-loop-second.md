# Alphabet loop: repetition and limited-word output

## What

Users try recursive character or word selection for text generation.

## How Jev fits

A–Z choices cycle alphabet after a sentence seed; top-200-word choices produce a short ungrammatical continuation.

## Why and impact

Direct counterexample to assuming typed token loops produce coherent prose.

## Limits and reuse

Specific vocabulary and prompts only; not native generation or general intelligence benchmark.
