# AskJev: interactive decision stories

## What

An educational demo explains Jev through five interactive stories.

## How Jev fits

Users add new details and see decisions change; the captured preview frames the experience around one question and additional context.

## Why and impact

Useful for teaching context-sensitive decisions and probabilities to nontechnical users.

## Limits and reuse

Demonstration does not establish probability calibration or a general reasoning benchmark.
