# Assignment rubrics: marking and coverage checks

## What

Users test marking student responses and ranking writing/sources against assignment criteria.

## How Jev fits

One pipeline uses an LLM to segment/classify submitted writing, then checks rubric sections and learning objectives with Jev.

## Why and impact

Promising overall coverage reports but mixed identification of specific deficient sections.

## Limits and reuse

Separate users and early reports; no representative grading agreement, bias or high-stakes assessment validation. Retain human review.
