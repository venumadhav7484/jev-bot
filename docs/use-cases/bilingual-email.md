# German and English email classification benchmark

## What

A user compares Jev with two Gemini models on 1,565 emails across ten categories.

## How Jev fits

Confidence is used to distinguish easier and harder classifications.

## Why and impact

Author reports slightly lower overall accuracy but 10–22× lower cost; all 737 predictions at at least 99% confidence match reference labels.

## Limits and reuse

Reference labels are AI-generated and not human-verified. The result does not imply a universal 99% automation threshold.
