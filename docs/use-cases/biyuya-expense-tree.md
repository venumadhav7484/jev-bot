# Biyuya: changing taxonomy traversal versus swapping models

## What

An author compares three expense-classification workflows over a purpose-based tree of 630 categories.

## How Jev fits

The comparison keeps test expenses fixed and separates a Jev replacement inside the old workflow from a redesigned level-by-level category traversal.

## Why and impact

On 3,600 AI-generated expenses, author reports 54.5% exact accuracy for the original model, 54.2% for the Jev swap and 58.5% for Jev with the new traversal. The design change, rather than the model swap alone, produced the reported gain.

## Limits and reuse

Synthetic generated expenses and category labels may not represent real users. Raw labels, full runner and calibration evidence were not supplied in this captured post; attached demonstration remains uninspected. Faster classification does not establish accounting correctness.
