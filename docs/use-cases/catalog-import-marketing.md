# E-shop catalogue import: 44,802 decisions and 769 unknowns

## What

A marketing consultant used Jev while importing almost 2,000 products of a brand portfolio into a client’s e-shop.

## How Jev fits

Jev made categorization decisions with an explicit “unknown” outcome; products it could not place were left for other handling rather than guessed.

## Why and impact

The author reports 72.5% of the catalogue categorized with no generative model or human, 44,802 decisions and 21 million tokens for $1.50, and 769 “unknown” answers.

## Limits and reuse

Only the post was reviewed; the full article with thresholds and failures was not. No accuracy audit of the categorized share.
