# Catan clone: Jev self-play and frequent trading

## What

Developer runs Jev agents against each other in a Catan-like game.

## How Jev fits

Low-latency decisions govern game actions, with frequent trading observed.

## Why and impact

Author reports enjoyable and reasonably effective prototype.

## Limits and reuse

No win-rate metric, self-play skill rating or rules audit; Rimworld and Dwarf Fortress integrations only proposed. Author reports repeated trade offers even after about ten rejections; improving memory of rejected offers remains an intended change.
