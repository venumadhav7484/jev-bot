# Dungeons & Dragons co-DM: measured wins and losses

## What

A co-DM integrates five judgment functions: table rules, contradictions, routing, art classification and fact triage.

## How Jev fits

The inspected report uses regex checks before Jev; routing remains in shadow mode and art changes require separate commitment.

## Why and impact

Corrected table-rule results average 88.0% recall and 95.4% precision over eight runs. Serial review took 4.2 seconds versus 131.5 seconds for the compared classifier.

## Limits and reuse

Earlier best-run figures were withdrawn; a contaminated comparison arm was discarded. Art classification scored 0.77 after prevalence adjustment versus 0.83 for always answering other. Savings were modeled, not observed spending. The page contains inconsistent contradiction-status statements and stale pricing text. No benchmark independently reproduced.
