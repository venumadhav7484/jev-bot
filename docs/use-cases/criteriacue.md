# CriteriaCue: evidence against role criteria, not a ranking

## What

A Chrome extension helps recruiters review a professional profile they are authorized to process against one to five criteria per role.

## How Jev fits

For each criterion, a Choice selects the evidence relationship and an exact supplied evidence block; the panel shows the source excerpt rather than generated quotations. The design keeps “insufficient evidence” separate from contradicting evidence, so missing information becomes a follow-up question.

## Why and impact

Keeps the human in the hiring decision: no candidate ranking, fit percentage or automatic decision.

## Limits and reuse

The walkthrough uses fictional data and simulated reviews; no live accuracy or latency demonstration. Hiring is a sensitive domain: typed output still needs human review, and profile text leaves the browser under the user’s key.
