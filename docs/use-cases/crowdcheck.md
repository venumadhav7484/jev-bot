# Crowdcheck: simulated audience reactions

## What

Explore reactions to a proposed post across synthetic personas.

## How Jev fits

Jev via Vercel AI Gateway supplies many small choices such as like, repost, ignore or block.

## Why and impact

The app advertises 10,000 simulated readers.

## Limits and reuse

Synthetic reactions are not validated forecasts of real audiences. Counts describe simulated personas, not surveyed people.
