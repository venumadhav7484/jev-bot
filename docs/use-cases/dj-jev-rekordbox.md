# DJ Jev: Jev makes the mixing decisions in Rekordbox

## What

An experimental macOS bridge lets Jev mix tracks in Rekordbox from a folder of music.

## How Jev fits

A native bridge reads the Rekordbox screen and drives keys and the pointer; Jev chooses timing, faders and bass from the observed state, optionally with offline audio timelines around both play positions, while code handles everything else.

## Why and impact

A verified 27 September trial ran for over eleven minutes with two autonomous handovers, EQ recovery and preparation of a fourth track, with no manual DJ control after start.

## Limits and reuse

Audible mix quality was not assessed; the trial ran without a controller, and Rekordbox must stay visible. Earlier attempts failed on timing limits. README is in Dutch.
