# Drum sequencer: 96 typed choices per bar

## What

Jev selects a complete drum pattern rendered by host software.

## How Jev fits

Six instruments each have 16 steps; 96 choices select rest, hit or accent in parallel.

## Why and impact

Demonstrates generating a structured musical artifact without native audio output.

## Limits and reuse

Author reports repetition and no temperature control. No music-quality evaluation; independent steps may need host constraints for rhythm.
