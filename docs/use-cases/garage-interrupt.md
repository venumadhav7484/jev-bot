# Garage Interrupt: which recalls matter to one vehicle

## What

An experiment judges NHTSA notices, bulletins, recalls and complaints against specific vehicles and decides which deserve an interrupt.

## How Jev fits

Jev is given one vehicle and one notice together and asked how relevant the notice is to that vehicle; the same notice gets a different answer per vehicle.

## Why and impact

Across 1,506 notices and 16 vehicles over 30 days: 24,096 decisions, 145 ms median, $1.74 in total. One pickup truck received 8 interrupts out of 1,506 notices.

## Limits and reuse

Author experiment; no labelled recall/precision figures shown. A missed recall is the costly error, so thresholds need checking against known recalls.
