# Internal classification: zero-shot Jev versus fine-tuned Qwen

## What

Author compares Jev with an existing fine-tuned 9B classifier on a private benchmark.

## How Jev fits

Claims zero-shot recall within about five percentage points at matched precision; roughly $70/month full-volume estimate and subsecond latency.

## Why and impact

Suggests possible lower training/operational burden, while baseline reportedly remains about three times faster.

## Limits and reuse

Preview truncated; dataset, full cost comparison, confidence intervals and private labels unavailable. Not proof Jev beats fine-tuned classifier.
