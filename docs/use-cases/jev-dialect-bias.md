# Dialect bias re-run: Jev as judge and as moderator

## What

A rerun of a 2024 study on dialect bias asks Jev the paper's judge questions and a content-moderation question about 2,019 matched tweet pairs in African American English and standard English.

## How Jev fits

Each pair carries the same meaning; Jev answers under four instruction sets, including a line that dialect is not evidence and a written moderation policy.

## Why and impact

On the paper's judge questions, plain Jev did not show the paper's penalty against African American English. As a moderator it removed AAE posts more often (17.8% vs 13.1%); instructions halved the gap but did not close it. Stating the writer's race moved answers more than dialect did (death-sentence rates 29.5% vs 62.7% on identical text).

## Limits and reuse

A limited adaptation of the paper; much of the moderation gap lines up with profanity and slurs in the source tweets, and the translation method cannot be separated from dialect. Never put protected attributes into state for decisions about people.
