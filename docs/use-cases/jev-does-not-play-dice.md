# Jev does not play dice: confidence on outcomes nobody can know

## What

An experiment checks whether Jev's reported probabilities reflect uncertainty when the true probability is known exactly: fair dice, fair coins and forecast documents.

## How Jev fits

Choice and Noul questions are asked about hidden fair draws and about documents that state a probability; reported probabilities are compared with the known values.

## Why and impact

Choice on a fair die reported 82.9% on average while being right 19% of the time; a fair coin got 92%. Noul was closer on a die (19.2%) but over-reported low probabilities (15% for 1 in 20). A document stating 45% came back as 6.6% via Choice and one stating 55% as 95.9%. A follow-up found Choice correctly returned 0% heads for a two-tailed coin.

## Limits and reuse

Prompt- and condition-specific; the author notes it does not establish that Jev probabilities are generally miscalibrated, and answerable tasks looked well calibrated. The lesson: do not read a Choice probability as a forecast of an inherently uncertain event; keep stated probabilities as numbers in code.
