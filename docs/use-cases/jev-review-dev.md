# Jev Review: staged Git-diff screening

## What

Small code-review workflow screens Git diffs and presents results in a local dashboard.

## How Jev fits

Jev performs initial judgments, then follows stronger signals through further stages.

## Why and impact

Potentially concentrates review effort on suspicious changes; no measured benefit in announcement.

## Limits and reuse

Repository and staged thresholds need inspection. No defect-recall or false-positive evaluation established. Source review: Staged judgments identify review leads, not verified defects. No compiler/static-analysis corroboration or independent detection benchmark supplied.
