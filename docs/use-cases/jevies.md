# Jevies: reviewing decision-model questions

## What

A helper project uses Jev to design and review other Jev decisions.

## How Jev fits

Reviewed diagram separates human/LLM drafting from typed question-type/form suggestions, Choice overlap checks, Score anchors/order and answerability/test-coverage review. Results saved and compared before revision.

## Why and impact

Potential feedback loop for schema and prompt quality.

## Limits and reuse

Diagram and repository workflow are not evidence that self-review guarantees correctness; correlated errors require independent labels and held-out evaluation.
