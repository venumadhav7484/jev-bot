# Jevometry: study output distributions under explicit assumptions

## What

A toolkit analyses geometry and sensitivity of Jev output distributions.

## How Jev fits

The author describes parameter perturbation, information-loss and dependency analysis, with Fisher-information and Cramér–Rao calculations under statistical conditions.

## Why and impact

Offers an exploratory way to inspect how typed output distributions move when system inputs or parameters change.

## Limits and reuse

Distribution geometry and theoretical bounds do not establish factual correctness or trustworthy action. The required statistical assumptions, numerical implementation and reproducibility remain unverified. Held as an experimental lead.
