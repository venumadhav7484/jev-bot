# Computer control with local vision and OCR

## What

Select interface actions without sending screenshots to Jev.

## How Jev fits

A local CoreML model segments controls and local OCR extracts labels; Jev receives text and selects actions.

## Why and impact

Separates perception from semantic decisions and can reduce image transmission in the described architecture.

## Limits and reuse

Text labels still leave the device. No evidence here establishes the privacy behavior of the entire application or icon-only reliability.
