# Magic-8-Jev: bounded-answer oracle

## What

A novelty oracle chooses among twenty opinionated answers.

## How Jev fits

Jev selects from a fixed response set instead of writing arbitrary prose.

## Why and impact

Simple demonstration of presenting typed decisions through a playful UI.

## Limits and reuse

Entertainment only; fixed answers do not establish prediction or factual accuracy. A reviewed screenshot selects drive rather than walk for a car wash50yards away; it supplies no raw probabilities or repeated-trial accuracy. Source review: Entertainment toy only; confidence is not a prediction probability. Provider alias and alpha decisions endpoint may change.
