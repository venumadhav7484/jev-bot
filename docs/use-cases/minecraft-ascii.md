# Minecraft: ASCII perception versus symbolic state

## What

A second Minecraft author describes a laggy Jev bot with structured state and ASCII views.

## How Jev fits

State includes position, hunger, dimension, inventory, nearby blocks/entities, cardinal collision hints and memories. Six 20x20 ASCII windows supplement it. Choice selects legal actions such as collect, fight or craft.

## Why and impact

Author finds explicit common-sense rules helpful, including solid blocks blocking movement and tool requirements. Discussion proposes Mineflayer symbolic perception.

## Limits and reuse

Author says ASCII-window perception does not work well. Need representation ablation and action/progress checks; no native vision or reliable navigation established.
