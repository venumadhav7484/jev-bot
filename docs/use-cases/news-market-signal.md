# News-to-market signal: unvalidated finance experiment

## What

A community member describes predicting stock direction from news; another frames the idea as encoding events for a trading model.

## How Jev fits

The discussion proposes semantic news judgments as model inputs. The author argues that native calibration removes additional calibration work; replies challenge that inference and request domain-specific checks.

## Why and impact

Useful primarily as a counterexample: apparently plausible output is not evidence of predictive usefulness.

## Limits and reuse

The author explicitly says the experiment was not backtested. A dramatic hypothetical event producing a plausible direction does not validate performance. Do not cite this as profitable trading, validated market prediction or a domain-calibration guarantee.
