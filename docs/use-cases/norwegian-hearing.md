# Norwegian public-hearing response classification

## What

An early-access test classifies 24 Norwegian salmon-tax hearing responses.

## How Jev fits

The inspected article uses 11 questions per document: two Choice tasks, eight topic Nouls and one substance Score. Reference labels come from two passes of the same frontier model.

## Why and impact

Author reports 20/24 stance agreement and median latency 0.32 seconds versus 2.7 seconds for DeepSeek without reasoning. Longer, more qualified instructions worsened argument agreement and calibration in this sample.

## Limits and reuse

Only 24 documents, with model-generated reference labels. Agreement is not human-verified correctness. Provider routing varied across the comparator runs. An escalation stage was proposed but not built; results were not reproduced here.
