# Playable NPCs: live directives and visible probabilities

## What

NPCs explore, gather resources or build when directed by a player.

## How Jev fits

Jev chooses next actions; user can override directive and inspect live options/probabilities.

## Why and impact

Makes semantic action policy inspectable during gameplay.

## Limits and reuse

Video exposes live directives, inventory and next-action probabilities; final sample still gathers resources toward house. No completed-goal distribution, consistency test or long-horizon planning benchmark.
