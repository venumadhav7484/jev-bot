# Policy-response simulation with 1,024 personas

## What

An experiment simulates responses to a changing policy discussion.

## How Jev fits

Jev judges reactions for synthetic personas carrying geographic, age and ideological markers.

## Why and impact

Enables many parallel hypothetical responses.

## Limits and reuse

Author explicitly says methodology is weak. Synthetic outputs are not public opinion data or validated digital twins of real people.
