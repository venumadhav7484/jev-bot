# Hacker News: adjustable semantic feed ranking

## What

Personalized Hacker News front page replaces one shared ordering.

## How Jev fits

Firecrawl extracts article text. Jev scores technical depth, drama, practical utility, AI slop, novelty and career value. Six sliders rerank cached scores locally, including offline.

## Why and impact

Lets readers express preferences over article qualities.

## Limits and reuse

No human relevance or retention benchmark. Scores reflect subjective rubrics; cached local reranking is distinct from new inference.
