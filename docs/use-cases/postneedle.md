# Postneedle: feedback while drafting a post

## What

Five readings give feedback on a draft’s style before publication.

## How Jev fits

One request asks Nouls about AI-like wording, humblebrag, engagement bait, lack of substance and conflict. False criteria describe near misses; code changes the UI indicator.

## Why and impact

A small example of contrasting criteria and batched feedback without generating replacement text.

## Limits and reuse

Tested by its author on roughly 40 self-written English/Spanish posts. A stylistic score cannot establish authorship or how readers will react. Reported latency/cost are environment-specific; broader validation is absent. September 21 update adds account-level text analysis and a Choice over authored verdict sentences. Author reports up to 0.37 drift with ten posts per request and a closer match with five; batch size must be tested on the actual workload. Quoted/media-dependent posts are excluded from the displayed aggregate. Neither style scores nor selected verdicts establish AI authorship.
