# Reaper: semantic review after normal checks

## What

A semantic linter reviews coding-agent work after tests and standard linters.

## How Jev fits

Jev checks semantic issues such as weakened tests, silent failures, scope creep and unnecessary abstractions.

## Why and impact

Adds checks that syntax and test execution alone may miss.

## Limits and reuse
