# SO-101: vision-to-decision robotics simulation

## What

Choose pick-and-place actions from structured observations.

## How Jev fits

Astra interprets overhead and wrist images, Jev returns typed decisions, and a local controller performs and checks movements.

## Why and impact

A clear perception-to-decision-to-control architecture.

## Limits and reuse

The described work is simulation; real-arm testing was future work. It does not establish physical safety or native Jev vision.
