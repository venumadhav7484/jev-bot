# Roulette Wars: game playtesting

## What

Automate playtests in a fictional betting-based combat game.

## How Jev fits

Jev selects units' bets from game risk and payout state; later posts explore player archetypes.

## Why and impact

The author reports three recorded wins against a 1000-HP boss in thirteen to fourteen rounds.

## Limits and reuse

These are game outcomes, not evidence of real-money gambling prediction. Three runs do not establish robust balance or strategy.
