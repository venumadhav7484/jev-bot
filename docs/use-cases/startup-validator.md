# Startup idea feedback while typing

## What

A prototype assesses startup ideas interactively.

## How Jev fits

Eight Jev judgments update as the user writes.

## Why and impact

Makes structured feedback fast enough for drafting.

## Limits and reuse

Judgments are not market research, revenue forecasts or proof an idea will succeed.
