# Story analysis with separate validation gates

## What

An early TypeSafe demo evaluates a technical blog post whose conclusion is implicit.

## How Jev fits

The discussion describes Score for a graded judgment and separate Noul checks for whether the prompt is a yes/no question and whether the relevant signal exists in the story. The author confirms story_signal is a Noul.

## Why and impact

Shows a reusable pattern: check question validity and evidence presence separately from the substantive answer.

## Limits and reuse
