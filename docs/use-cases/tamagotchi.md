# Virtual-pet care

## What

A demonstration connects Jev to a Tamagotchi.

## How Jev fits

Jev chooses care actions based on supplied game state.

## Why and impact

Shows recurring small decisions in a persistent simulation.

## Limits and reuse

Short recorded care loop shows Feed/Play/Clean/Sleep/Wait choices, changing state bars and accelerated time. No long-horizon survival distribution, optimality baseline or intervention audit.
