# Asset tweets: sentiment-to-action prototype

## What

Jev-backed system fetches recent asset tweets and returns BUY, SELL or HOLD.

## How Jev fits

Author reports about 577 BTC tweets, a BUY suggestion, strong HOLD probability and stop-loss display.

## Why and impact

Text sentiment feeds a financial decision interface. Later author cites one selected 15-hour BTC price rise after a BUY suggestion.

## Limits and reuse

No verified predictive edge, executed return, risk calibration or stop-loss validity. One selected price move does not validate strategy; retain as experimental counterevidence, not investment guidance.
