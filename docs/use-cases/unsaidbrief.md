# Unsaidbrief: missing decisions in product briefs

## What

A tool identifies ambiguity before someone builds an app from a vague brief.

## How Jev fits

Jev flags missing decisions and possible conflicts and exposes probabilities.

## Why and impact

Moves clarification earlier to reduce avoidable rework. No measured hours saved supplied.

## Limits and reuse

Questions and probabilities need evaluation against human-reviewed briefs. Flags are prompts for clarification, not proof that a brief is defective.
