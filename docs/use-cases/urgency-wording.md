# Urgency wording: expression versus business impact

## What

Two screenshots compare urgency judgments after changing business outcomes and wording.

## How Jev fits

A Noul asks whether the message expresses urgency. Both messages retain a request for help ASAP; one reports failed Stripe connection and lost sales, the other passing connection and gained sales.

## Why and impact

Screenshots show 98% and 90% true respectively. The author interprets the second answer as a failure; replies question that interpretation because urgency and positive outcomes can coexist.

## Limits and reuse

No objective labels or controlled wording ablation. Define expressed urgency separately from harmful impact or actual operational priority. These images do not establish a general keyword-trigger bug or validated classification accuracy.
