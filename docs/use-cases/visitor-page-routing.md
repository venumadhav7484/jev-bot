# Website visitors: intent-to-page routing

## What

Visitor describes what they need and is routed to a relevant website page.

## How Jev fits

Map natural-language visitor needs to existing website destinations and anchors; host navigates and preserves a short conversation trail. Recorded examples reach pipeline, security/SSO, support, contact and pricing sections.

## Why and impact

Converts open-ended help requests into bounded site navigation.

## Limits and reuse

Demo shows selected successful routes, not routing accuracy or improved conversion. No-match handling, route allowlisting, accessibility and sensitive-action confirmation require explicit design. Marketing numbers inside the mock CRM site are not measured Jev impact.
