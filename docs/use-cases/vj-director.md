# Real-time VJ scene direction

## What

Choose visual scenes during a DJ set.

## How Jev fits

Host extracts musical features and uses typed decisions to choose bounded scene families, palettes, effects and two-deck mixes. Recorded UI toggles Rules/Jev and Auto, accepts a textual artistic brief, and displays decision traces while host renders visuals.

## Why and impact

The author reports under 500 ms decisions and around $1 for a two-hour set.

## Limits and reuse

Musical feature extraction and rendering are separate from Jev. Author reports quality needs improvement. Recorded bright multicolor output after a dark/minimal brief illustrates imperfect artistic control. No flash-safety, beat-sync or latency benchmark; strict visual constraints require host enforcement.
