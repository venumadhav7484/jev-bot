# Real-time VJ scene direction

## What

Choose visual scenes during a DJ set.

## How Jev fits

Jev selects scene, palette, effects and mix once per musical bar; rendering and audio timing belong to the application.

## Why and impact

The author reports under 500 ms decisions and around $1 for a two-hour set.

## Limits and reuse

The author also notes visual quality could improve. Musical feature extraction is separate from Jev.
