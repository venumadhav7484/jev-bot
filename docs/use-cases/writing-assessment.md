# Sentence-level writing assessment

## What

A writing tool flags wordiness in text produced for Claude-built applications.

## How Jev fits

The author cycles through text with Jev to assess unnecessary wording. The linked page preview describes clarity, tone and sentence-level feedback.

## Why and impact

Author reports subjective improvement. This is a judge-and-revise loop, with text creation and revision handled elsewhere.

## Limits and reuse

No quality dataset, error rates or user study supplied. Subjective style checks should be configurable and advisory.
