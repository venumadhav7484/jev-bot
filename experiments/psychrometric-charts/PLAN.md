# Pilot plan (pre-registered 30 September 2026, before any Jev request)

## Question
Can Jev read a free-text psychrometric question well enough that code can answer it exactly? Jev must identify which property each given number is and which properties are being asked for.

## Pipeline under test (arm A)
1. **Code** extracts the numbers in the question, with any unit written after them (°C, °F, C, F, %, g/kg, kJ/kg, m³/kg). The parser knows units, not property names.
2. **Jev** (jev-1.13.0) answers one request per question:
   - `value_1` and `value_2`: Choice over all 7 properties (tdb, twb, tdp, rh, w, h, v), asking which property that number states. Units do **not** narrow the options.
   - `asked_<property>` × 7: Noul, "Does the text ask to find this property?"
3. **Code** converts units according to Jev's labels (°F → °C, % → fraction, g/kg → kg/kg) and calls `psychro.solve` at sea level. The asked set is every property with a Noul of at least 0.5, minus the two given ones.
4. There is no fallback. If the labels are the same property, form an invalid pair, or don't fit the unit, that question has failed.

## Questions (all generated by code, fixed seed 20260930, saved before the run)
- **200 English questions**, each stating exactly two values. They are generated from templates; the full run would add GLM paraphrases.
- **Pairs:** the 19 usable property pairs, spread evenly. Two pairs are excluded: dew point with humidity ratio (both carry the same information) and wet-bulb with enthalpy (ill-conditioned).
- **Styles, cycled:**
  - textbook: "Moist air has a dry-bulb temperature of 30 °C and…"
  - technician shorthand: "DB 30 WB 22 - DP?"
  - chat: "measured 30 degrees and 60% humidity, what's the dew point?"
- **Units:** °F for the temperatures in 25% of questions, °C otherwise. Humidity ratio in g/kg, enthalpy in kJ/kg, specific volume in m³/kg.
- **States:** dry-bulb drawn from 10–40 °C and RH from 20–90%, at sea level. Stated values are rounded:
  - temperatures to 0.1 or whole degrees
  - RH to whole %
  - humidity ratio to 0.1 g/kg
  - enthalpy to 0.1 kJ/kg
  - specific volume to 0.001 m³/kg
- **Ground truth:** `psychro.solve` on the values exactly as written, so rounding can't count against the pipeline.
- **What's asked:** 60% ask for one property, 30% for two, and 10% for "all the other properties".
- **Parser check:** before any Jev call, the parser must return exactly the two stated numbers and units for all 200 questions. If it doesn't, the parser is fixed first. That checks code, not Jev.

## Scoring and pass lines (both must pass)
| Line | Definition | Pass |
|---|---|---|
| P1 | Jev labels both given numbers with the right property | ≥ 194 of 200 (97%) |
| P2 | Final answer correct: the asked set matches exactly, and every returned value is within tolerance of the truth. Tolerances: ±0.2 °C for temperatures, ±1 point RH, ±0.2 g/kg, ±0.5 kJ/kg, ±0.002 m³/kg. | ≥ 194 of 200 (97%) |

- Also reported: accuracy of the asked set; breakdowns by pair, style and unit; every failure verbatim; confidence on wrong labels; median latency; token cost.
- Prompts are frozen. Nothing is re-worded or re-run after seeing results. Only transport failures (no answer) are retried.
- **Decision:** if either line misses, the idea is dropped with no post. If both pass, a separate plan is written for the full run (Hindi and Telugu, GLM paraphrases, gpt-6-astra baselines).

# Pilot v2 plan (pre-registered 30 September 2026, after v1 failed P2, before any v2 question is generated or sent)

## Why a v2
v1 failed P2 because 7 separate "is this property asked?" yes/no questions over-triggered on shorthand. v2 changes the pipeline's **design** and nothing else. The value-labelling instruction wording is unchanged from v1. Any write-up must report that v1 failed and why.

## Design changes
1. **Return all 7 properties**, the way a chart shows them, instead of guessing which ones were asked. A single Jev Choice (`asked_first`) picks the property to highlight: the first one asked for, or "all other properties". It affects highlighting only and has no pass line.
2. **Code narrows each number's options by its unit before Jev sees it** ("Jev may select among validated candidates"):

   | Unit written | Options Jev chooses from |
   |---|---|
   | °C, C, degrees, °F, F, degrees F | dry-bulb, wet-bulb, dew point |
   | % | relative humidity (set by code; no Jev question) |
   | g/kg | humidity ratio (set by code) |
   | kJ/kg | enthalpy (set by code) |
   | m³/kg | specific volume (set by code) |
   | no unit | all 7 |

   Nothing is inferred from nearby words; Jev reads those.

## Questions
- **200 fresh questions** from the same generator and templates as v1, with a new seed, **20261001**, saved and hashed before the run. The v1 questions are not reused.

## Scoring and pass lines (both must pass; the thresholds are unchanged)
| Line | Definition | Pass |
|---|---|---|
| P1 | Both given numbers end up with the right property (from Jev or from the unit rule) | ≥ 194 / 200 |
| P2 | All 7 returned properties within v1's tolerances of the truth, including unit conversion | ≥ 194 / 200 |

- **Reported without a pass line:** `asked_first` accuracy, breakdowns by style and unit, failures verbatim, latency and cost.
- **Note:** with all 7 returned, P2 can only fail through labelling or unit conversion. The solver is exact.
- **Decision:** if v2 misses either line, the idea is dropped with no post or demo. If it passes, build the `/psychro-data` demo and the post, video and article drafts, all reporting v1 and v2.

# Upload-reading test (pre-registered 30 September 2026, before the test images are generated)

The `/psychro-data` pipeline is under test, entirely in the browser apart from the Jev step:
1. OCR with Tesseract.js in up to 3 passes (black-only masks at thresholds 110 and 80, then the raw image).
2. The best pass is the one where tick labels form the widest dry-bulb row and humidity-ratio column.
3. Jev reads the OCR text and picks the chart kind and unit system.
4. Axes are fitted from the tick labels, then refined by matching the equations' RH curves to the chart's ink.

**Images:** 10 in all.
- Five FlyCarpet sea-level variants, derived in-page from the sample:
  - 902 px render (the development image, disclosed as such)
  - 677 px render
  - 1,300 px render
  - JPEG quality 0.7
  - 0.9× scale with 60 px white margins, like a screenshot
- Five charts drawn from the equations: sea level, 920 m, 1,500 m and 2,200 m, plus the 1,500 m chart as a JPEG (quality 0.7) at 720 px.

**Truth:** FlyCarpet's exact calibration from its SVG, or the drawn chart's own geometry, both scaled to the image.

**Error:** the worst difference over a grid of 10 dry-bulb values (0–45 °C, step 5) × 6 humidity-ratio values (2–22 g/kg, step 4), mapping each true point to pixels and reading it back.

**Pass lines**
- **U1:** at least 9 of 10 images read within ±0.3 °C and ±0.2 g/kg.
- **U2:** Jev names the chart "psychrometric chart" with SI units on at least 9 of 10.
- **Reported without a pass line:** pressure read from the chart text (within 1%); time per image.

**Limits, disclosed in advance:** these are clean digital images of two chart designs. There are no phone photos, perspective, glare or IP charts, so results don't carry over to those.

# Confirm-step test (pre-registered 30 September 2026, before any v3 question is generated or sent)

**Hypothesis, from the v2 analysis:** Jev's confidence separates misreads from correct readings. On v2, all 6 misreads had confidence of 0.70 or lower. If the pipeline asks the visitor to confirm any Jev reading below 0.75, silent wrong answers should almost disappear at a small cost in extra confirmations. The threshold came from the v2 data, so it is tested here on fresh questions and is **frozen at 0.75**.

**Setup:**
- 600 fresh questions from the same generator and templates, seed 20261002.
- The same v2 pipeline and prompts (src/pilot_v2.py payload), one Jev request per question.
- A number is "flagged" when Jev labelled it and its confidence is below 0.75. Numbers fixed by their unit are never flagged.

**Pass lines (all three must pass)**

| Line | Definition | Pass |
|---|---|---|
| C1 recall | Share of misread numbers that are flagged | ≥ 90% |
| C2 burden | Share of correctly read, Jev-labelled numbers that are flagged | ≤ 10% |
| C3 silent errors | Questions that get a wrong answer with **no** flag, as a share of all 600 | ≤ 1% (6 or fewer) |

**Also reported, without a pass line:**
- P1 and P2 on the fresh set: a replication of v2 at 3× the size.
- Misreads by style.
- The error sizes of any silent wrong answers.

**Decision:** if all three lines pass, add the confirm step to `/psychro-data`, with a saffron "Please check" on flagged readings. If any line fails, report it and don't add the step.
